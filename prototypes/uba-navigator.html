CBC — Ciclo Básico Común

The single door everyone enters through. Six cuatrimestral subjects — two shared by the whole university (Pensamiento Científico; Sociedad y Estado), two by area, two by career — before you set foot in a faculty. ~1 year, up to 3 to finish, and it can be run remotely (UBA XXI). Architecturally, it's the shared bootstrap every carrera links against.
